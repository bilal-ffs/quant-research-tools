📉 Drawdown
Drawdown is the decline from a portfolio’s peak to its lowest point before recovery, showing potential loss.

🧮 Formula

Drawdown = (Peak − Trough)/Peak

🎯 Importance

Highlights risk exposure

Shows worst-case scenarios

Key for comparing strategies

📊 Example

Peak = $100,000, Trough = $70,000 → Drawdown = 30%

🧾 Interpretation
Low (5–10%) → manageable risk

High (30%+) → volatile, risky strategy
